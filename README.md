<h1>Catan Board Game</h1>

<h2>Game Rule</h2>

<p>Max player: 2<br>
Objective: Be the first player to earn 10 victory points (VP) by building settlement, cities, road, and acquiring development cards or special bonuses.

<h3>1. Setup</h3>
•	Order: Players place their first settlement and adjacent road in normal order (1st -> 2nd) and start the game.<br>
•	Board Assembly: Randomly arrange hexagonal terrain tiles (e.g., forest, hills, pastures) in a square shape, with numbered tokens (2-12) on each tile (except for the desert).<br>
•	Harbors: Harbors are fixed to the map. So, for every new game, the harbors and their trades do not change.<br>
•	Starting Settlement:<br>
&ensp;&ensp;o	Players take turns placing 1 settlement and 1 road<br>
&ensp;&ensp;o	Collect resources from hexes adjacent to the settlement that you place.<br>

<h3>2. Gameplay</h3>
•	Resource Production<br>
&ensp;&ensp;o	If the total matches a number of a hex, all players with settlements/cities adjacent to that hex gain resources:<br>
&ensp;&ensp;&ensp;&ensp;	Settlement: 1 resource per hex.<br>
&ensp;&ensp;&ensp;&ensp;	City: 2 resources per hex.<br>
&ensp;&ensp;o	Exception: If a 7 is rolled:<br>
&ensp;&ensp;&ensp;&ensp;	Robber Activation: Move the robber to a new hex, blocking its resource production.<br><br>
•	Building & Trading<br>
&ensp;&ensp;o	Spend Resources to build on your turn:<br>
&ensp;&ensp;&ensp;&ensp;	Road (1 wood + 1 brick): Expands your network.<br>
&ensp;&ensp;&ensp;&ensp;	Settlement (1 wood + 1 brick + 1 wheat + 1 sheep): 1 VP.<br>
&ensp;&ensp;&ensp;&ensp;	City (2 wheat + 3 ore): Upgrades a settlement. 2 VP.<br>
&ensp;&ensp;&ensp;&ensp;	Development Card (1 sheep + 1 wheat + 1 ore): Grants knights, VP, or special abilities.<br>

&ensp;&ensp;o	Trading:<br>
&ensp;&ensp;&ensp;&ensp;	Dosmestic: Trade with players (any ratio, negotiated).<br>
&ensp;&ensp;&ensp;&ensp;	Maritime: Trade with bank at 4:1 (default), or better rates with harbors (e.g., 2:1 for sheep)<br><br>
•	Development Cards<br>
&ensp;&ensp;o	Playable only on your turn (except Victory Points, which are secret until winning):<br>
&ensp;&ensp;&ensp;&ensp;	Knight: Move the robber. Largest Army (3+knights) = 2 VP.<br>
&ensp;&ensp;&ensp;&ensp;	Progress: Year of Plenty, Roading Building, or Monopoly.<br>
&ensp;&ensp;&ensp;&ensp;	Victory Points: Counts toward 10 VP (hidden until game end).<br>
<h3>3. Winning the Game</h3>
•	The first player to reach 10 VP on their turn wins.<br>
•	Points come from:<br>
&ensp;&ensp;o	Settlement (1 VP)<br>
&ensp;&ensp;o	Cities (2 VP)<br>
&ensp;&ensp;o	Victory Point cards (1 VP each)<br>
</p>
